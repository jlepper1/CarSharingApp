Features

What the app does, screen by screen. For step-by-step instructions see the user guide.

  • Calendar – reservations
  • Trips – kilometres
  • Costs
  • Settlement
  • Cost-sharing rules
  • Cars and profiles
  • Working together
  • App on the phone, offline use

Calendar – reservations („Kalender“)

  • Shows who has reserved which car and when, as a month grid („Monat“) or as a list („Liste“) with „Kommende“ and „Vergangene“ reservations.
  • Filter by car („Alle Autos“ or a single car). Each person has a colour, so the calendar can be read at a glance.
  • A reservation has a car, start („Von“), end („Bis“) and an optional purpose („Zweck“).
  • No double bookings: two reservations of the same car can never overlap. This is enforced by the database itself, so it also holds when two phones save at the same moment.
  • Every reservation gets a permanent running number (Reservierung Nummer 12) so the family can refer to it. The number stays even when other reservations are deleted.
  • Inactive (sold) cars cannot be reserved.

Trips – kilometres („Fahrten“)

  • A trip records the car, the date, the odometer reading at the start („km-Stand Start“) and at the end („km-Stand Ende“), and an optional note. The distance is calculated, never typed in.
  • The start reading is pre-filled from the last recorded reading of that car. A forgotten trip therefore shows up as a gap instead of disappearing; the settlement warns about kilometres without a recorded trip.
  • Shared trips: a trip can belong to several people („Wer ist gefahren?“). All of them have equal rights and the kilometres are split equally between them. A person can remove only themselves from a trip („Mich austragen“); when the last person leaves, the trip is deleted.
  • No double-counted kilometres: the database rejects two trips of the same car whose odometer ranges overlap.
  • A monthly total („Kilometer im Monat“) is shown per person.

Costs („Kosten“)

  • Categories: Tanken (fuel), Laden (Strom) (charging an electric car), KFZ-Versicherung (insurance), KFZ-Steuer (vehicle tax), Reparatur, Wartung (service), Reifen (tyres), Sonstiges (other).
  • Each cost records who paid („Bezahlt von“), the amount, the date, optionally a car (or „Kein bestimmtes Auto“) and a note.
  • Fuel records the litres, charging the kWh, so the settlement can show consumption (l/100 km or kWh/100 km).
  • Yearly bills: with „Über einen Zeitraum verteilen“ a bill such as insurance or tax is spread day by day across its period (e.g. 1 Jan – 31 Dec), instead of landing completely in one month.
  • Money is always stored in whole cents, never as a floating-point number.

Settlement („Abrechnung“)

  • Per month („Monat“) or per year („Jahr“).
  • Totals: „Kosten gesamt“ and „Kilometer gesamt“.
  • Per car („Je Auto“): costs, kilometres, cost per km, and fuel or electricity consumption per 100 km.
  • Per person („Pro Person“): kilometres driven („Gefahren“), amount paid („Bezahlt“) and own share („Anteil“), plus the resulting balance.
  • Settling up („Ausgleich“): the app proposes who transfers how much to whom, with as few transfers as possible. After transferring, a transfer is marked „Als bezahlt markieren“; it then appears under „Erledigte Zahlungen“ and can be undone („rückgängig“). A payment counts on the last day of the settled period, so an open amount is not lost when the month changes.
  • Personal statement: tapping a person opens a line-by-line breakdown of their share — fixed costs split equally, usage costs split by km — with the reconciliation („Abstimmung“) of share, paid and balance.
  • Charts („Auswertung“): „Wer ist wie viel gefahren – mit welchem Auto?“, „Wer trug wie viel Kosten?“ and „Wer hatte wann welches Auto?“.
  • Export: „CSV“ for Excel (cells are protected against spreadsheet formula injection) and „Drucken / PDF“. On iPhone/iPad the page is rendered into a PDF and handed to the share sheet, because printing does not work in a home-screen app there.
  • Exact arithmetic: all shares are computed in whole cents; leftover cents are distributed by the largest-remainder method, so the shares always add up exactly to the total.

Cost-sharing rules („Kostenverteilung“)

Chosen under „Mehr“ and valid for the whole family:

Rule (as shown in the app)Meaning
Fixkosten gleich, Verbrauch nach km (default)Insurance and tax are split equally between all members; fuel, electricity, repairs, service and tyres follow each person's share of the kilometres.
Alles nach gefahrenen KilometernAll costs follow the share of kilometres. Whoever drives more pays more of everything.
Alles zu gleichen TeilenAll costs are split equally; kilometres are for information only.

Kilometre-based costs are split by each person's kilometres over all cars together, not per car.

Cars and profiles („Mehr“)

  • Cars: name, licence plate („Kennzeichen“), drive type („Verbrenner (Kraftstoff)“ or „Elektro“; hybrids count as fuel) and the odometer reading when the car was added.
  • Cars cannot be deleted, because that would change past settlements. A sold car is switched off („Auto ist aktiv“): it can no longer be reserved or driven, but late costs can still be entered and history stays complete.
  • Family („Familie“): list of all members with their colours.
  • My profile („Mein Profil“): each person sets their own display name and calendar colour. The six colours are chosen so that colour-blind people can tell them apart.
  • The app version and build are shown at the bottom of the „Mehr“ screen; „Abmelden“ signs out.

Working together

  • Everyone may edit everything: every active family member may correct or delete every reservation, trip and cost entry. Instead of locks, the app shows who created an entry and who changed it last; the database records this itself.
  • Live updates: changes made by others appear immediately, without reloading.
  • Private by default: only signed-in, active family members can read any data. There is no self-registration; accounts are created by the administrator.

App on the phone, offline use

  • Installable on the home screen of iPhone (Safari → Zum Home-Bildschirm) and Android (Chrome → App installieren). It opens full screen like a native app and updates itself automatically.
  • Offline, data that was already loaded can be viewed, but new entries require a connection.
