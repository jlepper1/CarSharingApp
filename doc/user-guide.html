Getting started

  1. You receive the app address, your e-mail login and a password from whoever manages the app. There is no self-registration.
  2. Install the app on your phone:
    • iPhone: open the address in Safari, tap Teilen → Zum Home-Bildschirm.
    • Android: open it in Chrome, menu ⋮ → App installieren.
  3. Open the app from the home screen, enter „E-Mail“ and „Passwort“ and tap „Anmelden“. You stay signed in.
  4. Go to „Mehr“ → „Mein Profil“, check your display name („Anzeigename“), pick a colour („Farbe im Kalender“) that nobody else uses, and tap „Profil speichern“.

The bar at the bottom has five tabs:

Forms open as a sheet from the bottom of the screen. Changes made by others appear automatically — no need to reload.

Reserve a car („Kalender“)

Make a reservation

  1. In „Kalender“, view „Monat“, tap the day you need the car. The reservations of that day are listed below the grid.
  2. Tap „+ Reservieren“.
  3. Choose the car („Auto“), start („Von“) and end („Bis“) date and time, optionally a purpose („Zweck“, e.g. „Einkauf“, „Urlaub“).
  4. Tap „Reservieren“.

If the car is already reserved in that time, the app refuses the reservation — pick another time or the other car.

View, change or delete

  • Switch to „Liste“ for „Kommende“ and „Vergangene“ reservations. With several cars, filter with „Alle Autos“ or a car name.
  • Tap a reservation to open it. Change the fields and tap „Änderungen speichern“, or tap „Reservierung löschen“ if the trip is cancelled.
  • Each reservation has a number (Reservierung Nummer 12) to refer to it in the family chat. The sheet also shows who created it and who changed it last.

Record a trip („Fahrten“)

Record every trip, ideally right after parking the car — this is the basis of the cost split.

  1. In „Fahrten“ tap „+ Fahrt“.
  2. Choose the car and the date („Datum“).
  3. Under „Wer ist gefahren?“ select yourself — and anyone else who shared the trip. The kilometres are split equally between all selected persons.
  4. „km-Stand Start“ is pre-filled with the last recorded reading of this car. Compare it with the dashboard: if the car shows more, someone forgot a trip — keep the pre-filled value (the gap is then visible in the settlement) or ask in the family.
  5. Enter „km-Stand Ende“ from the car's odometer (whole kilometres), optionally a note („Notiz“).
  6. Tap „Speichern“. The distance is calculated automatically.

Change a trip

  • Tap the trip → „Fahrt bearbeiten“ → adjust → „Änderungen speichern“.
  • „Mich austragen“ removes only you from a shared trip; the others keep it. If you are the last person, the trip is deleted.
  • „Fahrt für alle löschen“ deletes the trip completely.

The list shows „Kilometer im Monat“ per person; use the arrows at the top to change the month.

Enter costs („Kosten“)

  1. In „Kosten“ tap „+ Kosten“.
  2. „Art“: Tanken, Laden (Strom), KFZ-Versicherung, KFZ-Steuer, Reparatur, Wartung, Reifen or Sonstiges.
  3. „Auto“: the car the cost belongs to, or „Kein bestimmtes Auto“.
  4. „Betrag (€)“: e.g. 54,90. „Bezahlt von“: the person who actually paid (yourself by default).
  5. „Datum“ of the receipt.
  6. For fuel enter „Liter“, for charging „kWh“ — this gives the consumption per 100 km in the settlement.
  7. Tap „Speichern“.

Yearly bills (insurance, tax)

Tick „Über einen Zeitraum verteilen“ and enter „Zeitraum von“ and „Zeitraum bis“ (e.g. 01.01. – 31.12.). The amount is then spread day by day over the months instead of landing completely in the month it was paid.

Change or delete

Tap the entry → „Kosten bearbeiten“ → „Änderungen speichern“ or „Kosten für alle löschen“.

Settle up („Abrechnung“)

  1. Open „Abrechnung“, choose „Monat“ or „Jahr“ and use the arrows to pick the period.
  2. Read the totals and the section „Je Auto“: costs, km, euro per km and consumption per car.
  3. Under „Pro Person“ each person shows „Gefahren“ (km), „Bezahlt“ (what they paid) and „Anteil“ (what they should pay). Tap a person for their detailed statement, line by line; „Zurück zur Abrechnung“ returns.
  4. Under „Ausgleich“ the app suggests transfers, e.g. „Anna zahlt an Ben 42,10 €“. Transfer the money (bank, PayPal, cash).
  5. Then tap „Als bezahlt markieren“. The transfer moves to „Erledigte Zahlungen“. Marked by mistake? Tap „rückgängig“ next to it to mark it as open again.

When everything is settled the app says „Alles ausgeglichen – niemand schuldet jemandem etwas.“

A yellow hint like „Golf: 120 km ohne eingetragene Fahrt“ means the odometer readings have a gap: someone forgot to record a trip. These km are paid by everyone via the km share until the missing trip is entered.

Under „Auswertung“ three charts show who drove how much with which car, who carried how much of the costs, and who had which car when.

Export: CSV and PDF

  • „CSV“ (top right in „Abrechnung“) downloads the settlement of the selected period as a file for Excel or Numbers.
  • „Drucken / PDF“ prints the page or saves it as PDF. On iPhone/iPad the app creates the PDF itself („PDF wird erstellt …“) and then offers „Teilen / Drucken“ to save it to Files, send it or print it.

Settings, cars and your profile („Mehr“)

  • „Kostenverteilung“ — the split rule for the whole family. Discuss before changing it; it changes all settlements, including past ones.
    • Fixkosten gleich, Verbrauch nach km — insurance and tax equally, everything else by km (default).
    • Alles nach gefahrenen Kilometern — everything by km.
    • Alles zu gleichen Teilen — everything equally.
  • „Autos“ — „Auto hinzufügen“ with name, „Kennzeichen“, „Antrieb“ and „km-Stand jetzt“. Tap a car to edit it. When a car is sold, untick „Auto ist aktiv“: it can no longer be reserved or driven, but late costs can still be entered.
  • „Familie“ — all members and their colours.
  • „Mein Profil“ — your name and colour.
  • „Abmelden“ — sign out (e.g. on a shared computer).
  • The app version is shown at the very bottom.

Recommended routine

  1. Before driving: reserve the car in „Kalender“.
  2. After driving: record the trip in „Fahrten“.
  3. After refuelling/charging or paying a bill: enter it in „Kosten“ (keep the receipt).
  4. At the start of each month: open last month in „Abrechnung“, check for km gaps, transfer the suggested amounts and mark them as paid. Optionally save the PDF.

Questions and problems

Can I change entries made by someone else?
Yes. Every family member may correct or delete every reservation, trip and cost. The app shows who created and who last changed an entry — please let the others know.
The reservation is refused.
The car is already reserved during part of that time, or the end is before the start („Das Ende muss nach dem Beginn liegen.“), or the car has been deactivated.
The trip is refused.
Its kilometres overlap an existing trip of the same car (the km would be counted twice), the end reading is smaller than the start, or no person is selected. Check the readings in the trip list.
A car is missing in the selection.
It is deactivated („Kein aktives Auto.“). Re-activate it under „Mehr → Autos“ if that was a mistake.
I forgot my password.
Ask whoever manages the app; they can send a recovery e-mail or set a new password (see Database setup).
Can I use the app without internet?
You can look at data already loaded, but new entries need a connection.
The app shows „Einrichtung nötig“.
The installation is missing its database connection — a task for the administrator (see Installation).
Printing does nothing on the iPhone.
Make sure you have the current version (shown at the bottom of „Mehr“, 1.5.2 or later). Close and reopen the app to load updates.
